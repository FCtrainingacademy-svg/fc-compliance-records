Test procedure

  1. Turn off all appliances and the ECV. Connect the gauge at the meter test point.
  2. Let-by test: raise the pressure to 7–10 mbar, close the ECV and watch for 1 minute. Any rise means the ECV is letting by.
  3. Raise the pressure to the test pressure. Allow 1 minute for temperature to stabilise.
  4. Tightness test: record the pressure, then watch for 2 minutes and record the final pressure.
  5. Ask whether anyone can smell gas. Any smell of gas must be traced and repaired, whatever the gauge shows.

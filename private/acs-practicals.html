ACS Practicals

The practical tasks for your ACS assessments: core gas safety (CCN1) and CPA1, plus the boiler (CENWAT), cooker (CKR1) and gas fire (HTR1) appliance practicals. Method statement numbers are shown on each task. Open a task, work through the steps on a rig, tick them off, then say how confident you are. Print your record for your tutor to sign.

Before every practical

  • Say what you are doing and why as you go. The assessor marks what they see and what you explain.
  • Check your instruments first: gauge zeroed and checked, analyser in date and zeroed in fresh air, voltage indicator proved on a proving unit.
  • Visual check and safe-to-touch check of the installation before you touch pipework or the meter.
  • Have the manufacturer's instructions (MI) to hand and use their figures over general ones.
  • Record every reading. A test you did but didn't write down is a test you can't show.
Smell of gas or CO alarm? Turn off at the ECV, ventilate, no electrical switching or naked flames, get everyone out and call the Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999.

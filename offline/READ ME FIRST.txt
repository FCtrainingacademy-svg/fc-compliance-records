FC COMPLIANCE & LEARNER RECORDS – OFFLINE VERSION
FC Training Academy (LCL centre LC 447)

WHAT THIS IS
The same Compliance & Learner Records system, with the same tabs, but it runs from this folder
on your computer. You don't need Claude, a website or an internet connection to use it.

HOW TO OPEN IT
1. Open "FC Compliance.html" in Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge).
   On a Mac: right-click it > Open With > Google Chrome.
   Safari won't work, because it doesn't let web pages save into a folder.
2. The first time, click "Choose folder…" and pick THIS folder ("FC Compliance Offline").
   Chrome asks once to let the page view and save files here. Click "Edit files" / "Allow".
3. After that, open the file and click "Continue". Your records load straight away.

TIP: drag "FC Compliance.html" onto Chrome in your Dock, or bookmark it in Chrome
(press Cmd+D), so it opens with one click.

WHERE YOUR RECORDS ARE SAVED
- data/data.json – all records (staff, learners, centre checks, documents, attendance,
  off-the-job hours, EQA audit checklist). It saves automatically within a second of each change.
  The "Saved" line under the title shows the save status.
- data/backups/ – one automatic backup copy per day you use it.
- files/ – every PDF, photo or certificate you upload.
Don't rename or move files inside "files" and "data". The app finds them by name.

BACKUP AND OTHER COMPUTERS
This folder is in your Google Drive, so Google Drive backs it up whenever you're online.
To use it offline, right-click the "FC Compliance Offline" folder in Finder >
"Available offline" (Google Drive for desktop).
Only have the app open on one computer at a time. If two people edit at once, the last
save wins.

RESTORING A BACKUP
Close the app. Copy the backup you want from data/backups/ into data/,
then rename it to data.json (replacing the old one). Open the app again.

NOTES
- Reminders: "Add reminders to calendar" saves a calendar file. Open it to add every renewal
  date to your Mac or phone calendar.
- "Export CSV" saves spreadsheet copies for Excel.
- The "Folder" button lets you switch to a different records folder.
- The online version on claude.ai is separate. Changes made here don't appear there,
  and changes made there don't appear here.
